The reading system

The story stays in front.

Support should feel like a margin, not a wall. Each story can keep Persian central while Finglish, vocabulary, and cultural notes appear only when the reader wants them.

  • Level clearly shown before opening a story
  • Persian text remains the visual focus
  • Optional Finglish rather than permanent transliteration
  • Vocabulary tied to the actual story
